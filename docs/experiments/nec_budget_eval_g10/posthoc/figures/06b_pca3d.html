The same picture in three dimensions: do a state's nearest neighbours agree on whether “up” is a good move?

Each dot is one of the 1,282 probe states as embedded by a saved agent (seed 11, 250k steps), on its first three principal components. Blue: up is a good move; orange: it is not. Black rings: the 50 nearest probe states (in the full embedding space) of the example state, the black diamond. Panel titles: for the states where up is good, the average share of their 50 nearest states where up is also good; chance is 39%.
